Hi! I'm Harry — an incoming PhD student at the [MIT Operations Research Center](https://orc.mit.edu/), starting Fall 2026 under [Prof. Dimitris Bertsimas](https://www.mit.edu/~dbertsim/). My given name is *Charidimos*; "Harry" is what most people call me, and what you'll find me under on papers as well.

I recently completed my MEng in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the [National Technical University of Athens](https://www.ece.ntua.gr/en), where I wrote my diploma thesis with [Prof. Giorgos Stamou](https://www.ails.ece.ntua.gr/people/gstam) on adaptive multi-agent LLM systems for financial trading. I currently work as an AI engineer building agentic document-analysis systems.

My research interests sit at the intersection of **operations research and machine learning**. I'm especially drawn to language model agents — how to make them reason carefully, coordinate effectively in multi-agent settings, and produce decisions that are both performant and analyzable. More broadly, I'm interested in combining the rigor of OR with the flexibility of modern ML to build systems that are principled *and* practical.
